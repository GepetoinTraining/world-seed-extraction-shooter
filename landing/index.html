WORLD SEED

A browser game that runs on your own machine. Your character is yours: it is made in your browser, signed with a key that never leaves it, and kept on your machine.

What runs where

  • [■] On your machine: the game, your play, your save, and the model that writes for it.
  • [◆] On this hub: your account, and a check of your character's receipts: each signed, each following the last.
  • [ ] Never here: your play, your save, or a model key.

Make an account

3 to 32 letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores.

At least 10 characters. There is no email and no recovery yet, so keep it somewhere safe.

Play

  1. Get the game, and Node 22 or later.
  2. In the game's folder: npm install
  3. Then start it, pointed at this hub: 
    WS_HUB_URL=… npm run play
  4. Open http://127.0.0.1:3900. Under SYSTEM, THE HUB, sign in with the account above, register your character and send its receipts.

The model

The game checks your machine and says plainly whether it can run a model there. If it can, it uses Gemma through Ollama, or any model you choose. If it cannot, you can paste a key for one of the free options it lists. A key stays in the game's server on your machine, in memory, and is never sent here.
