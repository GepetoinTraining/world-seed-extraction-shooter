WORLD SEED

A browser game that runs on your own machine. Your character is yours: it is made in your browser, signed with a key that never leaves it, and kept on your machine.

What runs where

  • [■] In your browser: the game, your play, and your save.
  • [◆] On this hub: your account, and checks: your character's receipts, each signed and each following the last, and your world, whose new parts must keep the game's law.
  • [ ] Never here: your play, your save, or a model key.

Play

PLAY IN THIS BROWSER

Your save stays in this browser, on this machine. Under SYSTEM, THE HUB, sign in with the account above, register your character and send its receipts.

One tab holds the save at a time. Clearing this site's data clears the save, so back your avatar up from SYSTEM first.

The model

Played here, the game has no voice yet: it plays on without one. A model on your own machine (Gemma through Ollama, or any you choose) reaching this page is the next piece to be built. Until then it speaks only when started from its own folder with npm run play, which checks your machine and offers free options if it cannot run one. No key is ever sent here.
