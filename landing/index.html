WORLD SEED

A browser game that runs on your own machine. Your character is yours: it is made in your browser, signed with a key that never leaves it, and kept on your machine.

What runs where

  • [■] In your browser: the game, your play, and your save.
  • [◆] On this hub: your account, your character's birth, and checks: your character's receipts, each signed and each following the last, and your world, whose new parts must keep the game's law.
  • [ ] Never here: your play, your save, or a model key.

Your account

Asking this hub…

An account here is an e-mail and a password. The e-mail is how you get back in if the password is lost. This hub itself keeps neither: it keeps a handle and what your characters send it.

3 to 32 letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores.

At least 10 characters. This hub has no e-mail and no recovery, so keep it somewhere safe.

Play

Sign in above first. Every character is born on this hub's word, so the hub has to know whose it is before one is made.

PLAY IN THIS BROWSER

Your save stays in this browser, on this machine. Your character is registered here as it is made, and its receipts go up by themselves as you play.

One tab holds the save at a time. Clearing this site's data clears the save, so back your avatar up from SYSTEM first.

The model

Played here, the game speaks through a model of your choosing, and plays on without one if you choose none. It can be a model on your own machine (Gemma through Ollama, or any you choose), or your own key with a provider. Either is set up inside the game, under SYSTEM, THE VOICE, which shows every step before you run it.

Safari, and every browser on an iPhone or iPad, cannot reach a model on your own machine, so there a key is the only way. A key is sealed in this browser and is never sent here.

What this hub holds of you

    You can have all of it deleted: the account, its characters as this hub knows them, every receipt, and the e-mail and password with them. What is in this browser is yours and is not touched. It cannot be undone.
